Academic photo rows

.academic-photo-row and .academic-photo-frame pair a pinline photo frame with text. The outer frame aligns with the row’s left edge.

Academic frames have no padding, background, or shadow. In css-shared/src/common.css, --academic-photo-row-space-after starts at 2.25rem and controls each row’s bottom gap, including the final row, and the bottom margin of paragraphs immediately introducing rows.

--academic-photo-row-dividers is none by default. Set it to block to enable lines for all academic photo rows. Add data-divider to an individual row to show its line. Dividers sit within the existing gap and add no spacing.
