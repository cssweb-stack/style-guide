Buttons

Components reference.

Use one shared button family with selectable colors, treatments, icons, and arrows.

Buttons

One family with seven supported colors, three treatments, and optional icons or arrows. All have a minimum 44px target. Macro arguments: label, href, color, icon, arrow, treatment.
