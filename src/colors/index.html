Complete CSS Inventory

Shared colors are defined together in common.css. Guide-only presentation colors are defined at the top of style-guide.css. Swatches and displayed values read the loaded CSS variables.

Interpretation services use Black with --interpretation-band-opacity at 15% and --interpretation-heading-opacity at 10%. Shadows retain their separate pure-black base. currentColor inherits text color; transparent shows the background beneath it.
