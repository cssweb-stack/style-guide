Website Style Guide

One university. One college. A shared design foundation.

This guide supports University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa brand compliance and a consistent experience across College of Social Sciences websites. It brings shared typography, colors, layouts, and components together to help each site feel part of the university while presenting its own programs, people, and resources.
